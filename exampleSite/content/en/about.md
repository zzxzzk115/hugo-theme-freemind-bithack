This is the demo site of **freemind.bithack for Hugo**, a dark, terminal-style theme ported from
[hexo-theme-freemind.bithack](https://github.com/Ares-X/hexo-theme-freemind.bithack).

- Source: [github.com/zzxzzk115/hugo-theme-freemind-bithack](https://github.com/zzxzzk115/hugo-theme-freemind-bithack)
- Everything here is sample content. Try the search box (press `/`), the language switch, and reader mode (press `R` on a post).
